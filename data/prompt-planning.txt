Finally explain the rollback procedure, including which single configuration line changes when reverting to a quantized checkpoint, how to confirm the restored service is healthy, and what to check afterwards for silent regressions such as dangling containers, orphaned volumes, failed systemd units, or a stale driver overlay that no longer matches the kernel module. Be concrete and prefer commands over prose.
